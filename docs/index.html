LEARN THE WARNING SIGNS

Know your enemy.

Explore common digital threats and learn how to avoid them.

₹

UPI & payment scams

Fraudsters may impersonate buyers, send fake payment screenshots, or trick users into approving payment requests.

Stay safe

Verify transactions in your official payment app. A QR code or collect request should never be approved blindly to receive money.

✉

Phishing messages

Fake emails and texts imitate banks, delivery services, or familiar companies.

Stay safe

Check the sender and domain carefully. Visit official websites yourself instead of following unexpected links.

⌕

Fake job offers

Scammers may promise high earnings and demand registration fees or deposits.

Stay safe

Verify employers through independent official sources. Be suspicious of guaranteed earnings and upfront payment demands.

▣

Shopping fraud

Fake stores may advertise unrealistic discounts or never deliver purchased items.

Stay safe

Research the seller, return policy, and independent reviews. Use protected payment methods where possible.

!

OTP & impersonation scams

Someone pretends to be a bank official or authority and pressures you to reveal secrets.

Stay safe

Never disclose OTPs or PINs. End unexpected calls and contact the organization through its verified contact details.

⌑

Account takeover

Attackers try to gain access through stolen passwords, reused credentials, or social engineering.

Stay safe

Use unique passwords, a password manager, and multifactor authentication wherever available.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

Get help. Act quickly.

If you suspect cyber fraud, take practical steps and use official reporting channels.

!

Suspected financial cyber fraud in India?

Contact your bank or payment provider promptly, preserve relevant evidence, and report the incident through official channels.

Official cybercrime portal ↗ Cyber fraud helpline: 1930

Use 1930 for reporting suspected financial cyber fraud in India. Follow the official portal's current instructions.

✓

Secure your accounts

  • Contact your bank immediately if payment credentials or funds are at risk.
  • Change compromised passwords using the official website or app.
  • Revoke unfamiliar sessions and enable multifactor authentication.
  • Save messages, transaction IDs, and other relevant evidence.
  • Do not pay someone promising guaranteed recovery of lost money.
